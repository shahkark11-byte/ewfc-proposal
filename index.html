Dear Asma, Nahla and the EWfC team,

Many of the women you support already make and sell things: clothes, food, beauty products, crafts, art. What usually holds them back is not the product. It is the hours spent on photos, listings, messages and posting, and the difficulty of being found online at all.

That is exactly the kind of work I build tools for. I design AI systems that write product listings, make videos and handle the repetitive parts of running a small business. I would like to bring that to EWfC in a practical way: teach the women to sell on Etsy, eBay and Amazon, set up simple automations that save them time, and get their businesses seen.

I know EWfC has already run entrepreneurship and business-idea sessions with partners. This would build on that with the hands-on, digital side: real shops set up, real tools working, and the skills to keep going on their own.

What I can do

Four ways to help each woman's business.

Each one is chosen to fit the woman and her business. Someone selling homemade food needs different things from someone selling handmade jewellery, so we start with what she sells and who buys it.

Sell

Sell on Etsy, eBay and Amazon

Most of the women sell physical things. I can get them onto the big marketplaces and cut the time each listing takes.

  • Shops set up properly on Etsy and eBay, with Amazon Handmade where it fits
  • One product sheet turned into listings for each site
  • AI-written titles, descriptions and tags that buyers actually search for
  • Clean product photos from a phone picture
Save time

Automate the busywork

Small automations that run in the background, so she spends her time making and selling instead of typing.

  • WhatsApp Business quick replies and away messages
  • Order forms and bookings that collect details for her
  • One order list and a stock sheet that update themselves
  • Simple invoices and an AI assistant for everyday writing
Get seen

Get found online

A great business that nobody can find does not sell. We put each one on the map.

  • Google Business Profile so she shows up on Maps and in search
  • Instagram and TikTok set up and planned properly
  • A simple website of her own, where it makes sense
  • A posting plan she can keep up in minutes a day
Stand out

Promo videos in her own language

Short motion-graphic videos like the ones on this page, for her shop and social media.

  • Made in English and in her customers' language, such as Arabic, Urdu or Farsi
  • Ready for Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp status and shop pages
  • Taught as a workshop so she can make simple ones herself

Questions you might have

Good to know.

Do the women need to be good with technology?

No. If she can use WhatsApp, she can do this. Every session is hands-on and goes at the group's pace, with picture handouts to take home.

Do they need a laptop?

A smartphone is enough for most of it. A laptop helps for setting up shops and listings, so it would be useful if the centre has one or two we can share in sessions.

What about passwords and privacy?

Each woman creates and owns her own accounts. I never keep anyone's passwords or personal details, and nothing is set up in my name.

Can sessions be in other languages?

I teach in English, with plain words and lots of visuals. Handouts and AI prompts can be prepared in Arabic, Urdu, Farsi or other languages, and volunteers or staff are welcome to help translate in the room.

Will this guarantee sales?

No one can promise that honestly. What I can promise is that each woman leaves with her business properly online, tools that save her time, and the skills to keep improving it.
